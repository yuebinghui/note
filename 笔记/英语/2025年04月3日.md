## 写作

have an impact on...表示对……有影响
the shaping of...意为……的塑造。

Social media have profound impact.

*Social media* has had a profound impact on *the shaping of* teenagers' values, both positive and negative.

Social media has exerted a far-reaching influence on the formation of adolescents' values, with both positive and negative aspects.

社交媒体对青少年的价值观塑造产生了深远影响，既有积极的也有消极的。
