participation in...表示参与……；
accumulate 意为积累。

社交媒体的兴起导致信息传播速度加快，同时也引发了一些问题，如虚假信息的传播

*The rise of social media* has led to *the acceleration of information spread* and, at the same time, has caused some problems, such as *the spread of false information*

lead to 表示导致；
at the same time 表示同时；
such as...用于举例

*The rise of social media* has led to the quickening of information spread and, at the same time, has caused some problems, such as the spread of false information

The emergence of social media has given rise to the acceleration of information dissemination and, meanwhile, has triggered some issues, such as the propagation of false information.
