## 写作

电子游戏在年轻人中非常流行，但过度沉迷会对身心健康造成危害。

be popular among...表示在……中流行，
but 连接两个并列句，
indulgence in...意为沉迷于……

*Video games* are very popular among young people, but excessive indulgence in them can harm *physical and mental health*

---

社区活动有助于增强邻里关系，营造和谐的社区氛围。

contribute to doing sth.表示有助于做某事，
strengthen 意为加强，
create 意为营造

*Community activities* contribute to strengthening neighborhood relations and creating a harmonious *community atmosphere*.
