## 词组

###### brake ❌ ❌ ❌ ✅ 

n. 刹车

###### handbrake ❌ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

n. 手刹车；手闸

###### be a handbrake on sth. ❌ ❌ ✅ ✅✅ 

限制/阻碍某事物发展

###### at stake / on the line ❌ ✅ ✅✅ 

处于危险中

###### place sth. at risk ✅ ✅ ✅✅ 

使某事物处于危险之中
Consequently—and paradoxically—laws introduced to protect the jobs of ordinary workers may be *placing* those jobs *at risk*.
因此，矛盾的是，为保护普通员工的工作岗位而出台的法律可能反而会使他们的岗位岌岌可危。

###### be immune from sth. ❌ ✅ ✅✅ 

不受......影响
Defendants *are immune from* trial by their peers.
被告免受于同等地位的人的审判

###### employment protection law ✅ ✅ ✅✅ 

就业保护法

###### be cautious about doing sth. ✅ ✅ ✅✅ 

谨慎做某事
*are cautious about* intellectual investigation.
对学术研究持谨慎态度。
