## 背诵及默写句子

###### techniques ❌ 

n. 技术；方法；

Two main techniques have been used for training elephants, which we may call
respectively the tough and the gentle.
训练大象主要有两种方法，我们可以分别称之为“粗暴法”和“温和法”。

###### beating ❌ 

v. 打（beat 的 ing 形式）

The former method simply consists of setting an elephant to work and beating him until he does what is expected of him.

前一种方法仅仅是让大象开始工作，并不断地打它，直到它按照预期完成任务。
