## 词组

###### give rise to ✅ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

引起;导致

###### place strain on ❌ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

使.....紧张
For example, a high growth rate *placed* great *strains on* the existing structures of governance.
例如，高增长率给现有的治理结构带来了巨大压力。

###### in the wake of sth. ❌ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

随......之后而来
the action comes *in the wake of* a series of recent decisions
在最近的一系列决定之后采取行动

###### lock sth. away ❌ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

把...锁起妥藏
Museums *locked* some of them *away* in the dark
博物馆就把其中一些锁了起来藏于暗处

###### at stake ❌ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

有风险
And more is *at stake* here than individual objects.
此外，这里有风险的不仅仅是个别物品。
