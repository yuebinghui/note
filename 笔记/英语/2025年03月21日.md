B 产品的市场份额在 2020 年急剧下降，之后保持稳定。

market share of Product B drop

*The market share of Product B* dropped sharply in 2020 and then remained stable.

There was a dramatic decline in the market share of Product B in 2020, after which it maintained a stable state.
