## 词组



###### in effect ✅ ✅ ✅ 

实际上;事实上

The Court in effect support the medical principle of double effect.
实际上法院认可了双效医疗原则。

###### a bunch of ✅ ✅ ✅ 

大量;大批

###### put sth. together ❌ ✅ ✅ 

整理出，拼凑，凑成

The key messages that have been *put together* for World Environment Day do include a call for governments to enact legislation to curb single-use plastics.
在为世界环境日整理的关键信息中，的确包括呼吁各政府制定法律来限制一次性塑料制品。

###### do one's bit ❌ ✅ ✅ 

做贡献，尽力

###### bold ❌ ✅ ✅ 

adj.大胆的；粗体的

satisfying a need to have "(do) *done our bit*"without ever progressing onto bigger,bolder,more effective actions.
满足人们“已尽本分”的（心理）需求，使人们不愿意进一步采取更重大、更果敢、更有效的行动。

###### hold...to account ❌ ✅ ✅ 

追究...的责任

After the project failed, the team was *called to account* by the board.
项目失败后，团队被董事会追究了责任。

## 写作任务

###### popularization ❌ ✅ ✅ 

大众化；普及

###### dissemination ❌ ✅ ✅ 

n. 宣传，散播；

互联网的普及使得信息传播变得更加迅速和便捷。

The Internet **make informatino transmission** more rapid and convenient.

The *popularization* of the internet has *made* the *dissemination* of information more rapid and convenient.

The *prevalence* of the Internet has *rendered* the *spread* of information
much faster and more convenient.

---

###### teamwork ❌ ✅ ✅ 

n. 团队合作；协力

团队合作是实现共同目标的关键因素。

**Team work** is **the key element** of **fulfilling the mutual flag**.

*Teamwork* is a *key factor* in *achieving common goals*.

*Collaboration* is a *crucial element* in *attaining shared objectives*.
