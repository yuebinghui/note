## 写作

传统文化的保护对于维护民族的文化多样性和传承民族精神具有重要意义。

be of great significance for...表示对……有重要意义；
maintaining...和 inheriting...为并列的动名词短语作 for 的宾语。

The protection of traditional culture is great significance cultural diversity.

*The protection of traditional culture* is of great significance for *maintaining* the cultural diversity of the nation and *inheriting* the national spirit

The preservation of traditional culture is crucial for safeguarding the cultural variety of the nation and passing on the national spirit.

坚持锻炼和保持积极的心态有助于提高身体免疫力和应对压力

 Persisting in exercise contribute to improving physical.

persist in doing sth.表示坚持做某事；
contribute to doing sth.表示有助于做某事。

 *Persisting in exercise* and *keeping a positive attitude* contribute to improving physical immunity and coping with stress.

Sticking to exercise and maintaining an optimistic mindset are conducive to enhancing physical immunity and dealing with stress.
