## 词组

###### be the envy of ❌ 

 成为...羡慕的对象 
A is the envy of B    B羡慕A
The UK's GDP has *been the envy of* the western world.
英国的GDP一直是西方世界羡慕的对象。

###### shed light on ❌ 

为...提供解释;使...较容易理解
These reports *shed light on* some grim realities.
这些报告揭示出一些严酷的现实。

###### contribute to ❌ 

有助于;促成，促使
Enough sleep will *contribute to* longevity.
足够的睡眠有益于长寿。

###### hold one's nose ❌ 

捂住鼻子；勉强克服自己的厌恶感
*hold its nose* at the ethic of his conduct
对他行为的道德问题掩鼻而不屑

###### in return for ❌ 

作为对...的回报
I sent him a present in return for his help.
我送给他一份礼物以回报他的帮助。

###### sculpture ❌ ✅✅

[动] 雕刻    [名] 雕刻品；雕塑品；雕刻

###### fat forms, shape in only two dimensions ❌ ✅✅

平面形态、二维形状

###### appreciation of  ❌ ✅✅

欣赏，对……的欣赏 

That is perhaps why sculpture *has been described* as the most difficult of all arts; certainly it is more difficult than the arts *which* involve appreciation of fat forms, shape in only two dimensions.

这或许就是为什么雕塑会被认为是所有艺术中最难的一种；当然，它确实比那些只涉及欣赏平面形态、二维形状的艺术更难。

That is perhaps why sculpture has been described as the most difficult of all arts; certainly it is more difficult than the arts which involve appreciation of fat forms, shape in only two dimensions.



###### as it were ❌ ❌ ❌ 

似乎就是；好像（可以说，在某种程度上）

######  as if ❌ ✅ ✅

好像，仿佛

###### enclose ❌ ✅ ✅

[动] 封入；围住；附上

He gets the solid shape, as it were, inside his head —he thinks of it, whatever its size, as if he *were holding* it completely enclosed in the hollow of his hand.





他仿佛能在脑海中清晰地构想出这个立体形状——无论其大小如何，他都仿佛在手中将其完全握住，仿佛它就被包裹在他掌心的空腔里。



He gets the solid shape, as it were, inside his head —he thinks of it, whatever its size, as if he were holding it completely enclosed in the hollow of his hand.
