###### make the most of ❌ ❌ ❌ ❌ 

充分利用;尽情享受

We must *make the most of* our spare time.
我们必须最大限度地利用我们的业余时间。

###### drive down ❌ ❌ ❌ ❌

## 背诵及默写句子

###### disunited ❌ ❌ ❌ ✅ 

adj. 分裂的；不和的

###### essentially ❌ ❌ ❌ ✅ 

adv. 本质上，根本上；

If a nation is essentially disunited, it is left to the government to hold it together.
如果一个国家从根本上处于分裂状态，那么就需要政府来维持它的统一。



If a nation is essentially disunited, it is left to the government to hold it together.

###### take responsibilities ❌ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

承担责任

###### exercise initiative ❌ ❌ ❌ ✅ 

发挥主动性

###### fall within one's sphere  ❌ ❌ ❌ ❌ 

属于某人的范围

###### administrative overheads ❌  ❌ ❌ ✅ 

管理费用

If they can each be trusted to take such responsibilities and to exercise such initiative as falls within their sphere, then administrative overheads will be low.



如果他们都值得信赖，能够承担起各自的责任并在自己的领域内发挥主动性，那么行政开支就会降低。
