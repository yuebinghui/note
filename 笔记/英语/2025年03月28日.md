## 写作

人们应该树立正确的消费观念，避免盲目跟风消费。

###### establish ❌ ✅ ✅✅ 

意为树立；

###### avoid doing sth. ❌ ✅ ✅✅ 

表示避免做某事。

People should create correct consumption and avoid consumption;

People should establish *a correct consumption concept* and *avoid blindly following* the trend of consumption.


People ought to foster a proper consumption ideology and refrain from mindlessly following the consumption trend.


---

科技的进步为医疗领域带来了新的突破和机遇

###### breakthrough ❌ ✅ ✅✅ 

n. 突破，重大进展

###### bring sth. to... ❌ ❌ ❌ ✅ 

表示给……带来某物。

The progress of technology has brought new opportunities;

The progress of technology *has brought* *new breakthroughs and opportunities* to the medical field.

*The advancement of technology* has introduced new breakthroughs and chances to the medical sector.
