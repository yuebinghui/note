## 写作任务

科技的发展为人们提供了更多的娱乐选择。

The development of technology for people 

*The development of technology* *has provided* people with *more*
*entertainment options.*

The advancement of technology has furnished people with more
entertainment alternatives.

Good time management contribute to enhence the efficiency of studying.

*Good time management* *contributes to* *improving learning efficiency*.

Effective time management is conducive to enhancing learning productivity.
