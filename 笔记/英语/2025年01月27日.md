## 词组

###### turn sth. on its head  ❌ 

使人完全改变思路;使人从反面思考
The lawyer quickly *turned* his main defense argument *on its head*.
那位律师很快完全改变了主要的辩护思路。

###### be staring sb. in the face ❌ 

明摆着;显而易见
The mistakes of the past *stare* us *in the face*.
过去的错误就摆在我们的面前。

###### pick up ❌ 

增加;提高

###### stem from sth.  ❌ 

是...的结果;起源于;根源是
His headache *stemmed from* vision problems.
他的头痛是由视力问题引起的。

###### around the clock ❌ 

日夜不停;夜以继日
We worked *around the clock* to finish the job.
我们为完成任务夜以继日地工作。

be staring sb. in the face 这个词组在 2018 年考研英语二 Text 1 中
出现过意为摆在某人面前，明摆着。



## 背诵及默写句子

###### fashion ❌ 

[名] 时尚；流行；  [动] *制作；把…塑造成*

###### be fashioned after ❌ 

（仿照…… 制作 / 塑造）

If our educational system *were fashioned after* its bookless past we *would have* the most democratic form of 'college' imaginable.

如果我们的教育体系仿照其没有书籍的过去来塑造，那么我们将会拥有可以想象到的最民主的“大学”形式。



If our educational system were fashioned after its bookless past we would have the most democratic form of 'college' imaginable.

###### during the past centuries ❌ 

在过去的几个世纪期间

###### happy few ❌ 

幸运的少数人

*This shows* how long it was before we deemed it necessary to make sure *that* all our children could share in the knowledge accumulated by the 'happy few' during the past centuries.

这显示了在我们认为有必要确保所有孩子都能分享过去几个世纪里“少数幸运儿”所积累的知识之前，我们经历了多么漫长的时间。



This shows how long it was before we deemed it necessary to make sure that all our children could share in the knowledge accumulated by the 'happy few' during the past centuries.
