## 词组

###### a case in point  ❌ ✅ ❌ ✅ 

明证;恰当的例证
A pair of bills provide *a case in point*.
两项法案提供了一个恰当的事例。

###### set aside ❌ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

拨出，留出
*set aside*  percent of boards seats for women
给女性留出%的席位

###### frown on ❌ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

不赞成;不同意:不许可
The US Supreme Court *frowns on* sex-based classifications.
美国最高法院不赞同基于性别的分类。

###### private sector  ❌ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

私营部门

###### refer to... as...  ❌ ✅ ✅ ✅ 

提到;谈及;说起
[短语] 把…称作；把…当做
As the brain fades, we *refer to* these occurrences *as* "senior moments".
随着大脑逐渐衰退，我们把发生的这些事件称为“瞬间痴呆”。

## 写作任务

社交媒体的广泛使用带来了一些问题，如信息泄露和网络暴力。

Widespread use of social media has bring some problem, such as information leak and internet violence.

*The widespread use of social media* has brought about some problems,
such as *information leakage* and online violence.

The extensive use of social media *has given rise to* *several issues*, *like*
information disclosure and *cyber violence*.
