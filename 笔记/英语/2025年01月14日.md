## 背诵及默写句子

###### mud ❌ ✅ ✅

n. 泥，淤泥

###### silt  ❌ ❌ ❌ 

n. 淤泥 v. （使）淤塞

###### deposit ❌ ❌  ❌ 

v. 放下，放置；储蓄；；沉淀； n. 沉积物；押金

###### and the like ❌ ✅ ✅

等等，诸如此类

*It is* animals and plants which lived in or near water *whose* remains are most likely to be preserved, for one of the necessary conditions of preservation *is* quick burial, and *it is* only in the seas and rivers, and sometimes lakes, where mud and silt have been continuously deposited, *that* bodies and the like can be rapidly covered over and preserved.

这句话是一个比较复杂的主从复合句，首先主句：It is animals and plants which lived in or near water whose remains are most likely to be preserved 这是一个强调句，强调的是“animals and plants which lived in or near water”（生活在水中或水边的动植物）其中“which lived in or near water”是定语从句，修饰“animals and plants”，说明这些动植物的生活环境“whose remains are most likely to be preserved”是另一个定语从句，修饰“animals and plants”，表示这些动植物的遗体最有可能被保存下来



最有可能被保存下来的是那些生活在水中或水附近的动植物遗骸，因为快速埋藏是保存的必要条件之一，
而只有在海洋、河流（有时也包括湖泊）中，由于泥土和淤泥的不断沉积，动植物遗骸等才能被迅速覆盖并保存下来。



It is animals and plants which lived in or near water whose remains are most likely to be preserved, 
for one of the necessary conditions of preservation is quick burial, 
and it is only in the seas and rivers, and sometimes lakes, where mud and silt have been continuously deposited, that bodies and the like can be rapidly covered over and preserved.

###### be meant to be ❌ ✅ ✅ 

本应该发生但最终没有实现

意思是 “本应是；原本打算成为”，在句中用来描述原本计划的时长设定，What was meant to be an eight-day stay 即 “原本打算只待八天的（行程 / 安排）”。

表示 “接近…… 的界限 / 刻度”，这里是说接近三个月这个时间点，也就是 is approaching the three-month mark。
