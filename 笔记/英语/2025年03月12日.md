## 词组

###### work on  ❌ ✅ ✅✅ 

研究;调查

###### be bound up with sth.  ❌ ✅ ✅✅ 

和某事密切相关
the recognition given to him for his work *were all bound up with* the class politics of the army at the time.
对他工作的认可都与当时军中的阶级政治密切相关

###### in one's own right  ❌ ✅ ✅✅ 

凭本身的品质/凭借自身的能力、资格或地位
Local geological studies represented worthwhile research *in their own right.*
区域地质学研究本身就象征着有价值的科研。

###### make one's way ❌ ❌ ❌ ✅ 

 上进;成功

so his attempts to *make his way* up the promotion ladder *speak volumes about* British society.
他朝着晋升阶梯向上的努力也充分说明了英国社会。

###### speak volumes about sth.  ❌ ❌ ❌ ✅ 

充分说明...:足以反映
