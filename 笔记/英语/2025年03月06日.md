###### weigh up ❌ ✅ ✅  ✅ 

认真考虑;权衡;斟酌
As the latest crop of students pen their undergraduate application form and *weigh up* their options...
当最新一批学生填写他们的本科申请表并*权衡*他们可选择的道路时...

###### make sense ✅✅ ✅  ✅ 

合乎情理;...是明智的
Figuring out a way to accelerate that transition would *make sense* for them.
对他们而言，找出一种能够加速这一转变的方法是明智之举。

###### be/ have done with ❌ ✅ ✅  ✅ 

不再操心;完成;结束
It is unlikely that Generation Z will *be done with* education at  or .(have/be done with)
Z世代不可能在或岁就结束教育
