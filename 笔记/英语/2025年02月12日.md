## 词组



###### fend off ❌ ✅ ✅

抵挡，挡开，避开
Healthy trees are also better able to fend off insects.
健康的树木也能更好地抵御昆虫。

###### in the event of  ❌ ✅ ✅

[短语] 万一；如果…发生；
Even in the event of a fire, fewer trees are consumed.
即使发生火灾，消耗的树木也更少。

###### lock ...away ❌ ✅ ✅

[短语] 把…锁起来；封存
Museums locked some of them away in the dark.
博物馆把其中一些锁了起来藏于暗处。

###### under way ❌ ✅ ✅

已经开始;在进行中
New research on transportation biofuels is already under way.
有关生物燃料运输的新实验已在进行中。

###### all along ❌ ✅ ✅

自始至终，一直
The plot is capturing my attention all along.
这个情节一直吸引着我的注意力.



## 背诵及默写句子

Ice is only as safe as the water *from which it is made*, and should not be put in drinks unless it is known to be safe.

冰的安全程度仅与其制成的水的安全程度相当，除非确定其安全，否则不应将其放入饮料中。

###### tap ❌ ✅ ✅

n.水龙头，v.轻拍

######  tap water  ❌ ✅ ✅

自来水

If no other safe supply can be obtained, tap water *that is too hot to touch* can be left to cool and is generally safe to drink.

如果无法获得其他安全的饮用水源，可以让滚烫得无法直接饮用的自来水冷却后再喝，这样的水通常是可以安全饮用的。

---

Ice is only as safe as the water from which it is made, and should not be put in drinks unless it is known to be safe.

If no other safe supply can be obtained, tap water that is too hot to touch can be left to cool and is generally safe to drink.

## 写作任务

###### popularization ❌ ✅ ✅ 

大众化；普及

###### dissemination ❌ ✅ ✅ 

n. 宣传，散播；

互联网的普及使得信息传播变得更加迅速和便捷。

The Internet **make informatino transmission** more rapid and convenient.

The *popularization* of the internet has *made* the *dissemination* of information more rapid and convenient.

The *prevalence* of the Internet has *rendered* the *spread* of information
much faster and more convenient.

---

###### teamwork ❌ ✅ ✅ 

n. 团队合作；协力

团队合作是实现共同目标的关键因素。

**Team work** is **the key element** of **fulfilling the mutual flag**.

*Teamwork* is a *key factor* in *achieving common goals*.

*Collaboration* is a *crucial element* in *attaining shared objectives*.
