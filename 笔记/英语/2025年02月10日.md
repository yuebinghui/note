## 词组

###### look at ❌ ✅✅ ✅ 

研究

###### habitation ❌ ✅✅ ✅ 

n. 居住；住所

They have *looked at* half a century of data from fisheries around the world.
他们研究了全球各个渔场半个世纪的数据。

###### run on ❌ ✅✅ ✅ 

依靠;依...而定;取决于

###### soak up ❌ ✅✅ ✅ 

 吸收，吸掉

Sponges readily soak up water.
海绵容易吸水。

###### count on  ❌ ✅✅ ✅ 

依赖，依靠，指望

We cannot count on other people to help us.
我们不能指望别人来帮助我们。

###### strike a balance ❌ ✅✅ ✅ 

找到折中办法;平衡

Strike a balance between work and pleasure.
在工作和快乐中求得平衡。






## 背诵及默写句子

###### rim ❌ ✅✅ ✅ 

[名] 边；缘；边界

*Make sure that* all bottles are opened in your presence, *and that* their rims are clean and dry.

确保所有瓶子都在你的面前打开，并且它们的瓶口干净且干燥。

###### Portable boiling elements ❌ ✅✅ ✅ 

便携式加热元件

###### voltage ❌ ✅✅ ✅ 

n. 电压，伏特数

###### volt ❌ ✅✅ ✅ 

n. 伏特（电压单位）

Portable boiling elements that can boil small quantities of water are useful when the right voltage of electricity is available.

当有合适电压的电源可用时，能够煮沸少量水的便携式加热元件非常有用。

---



Make sure that all bottles are opened in your presence, and that their rims are clean and dry.

Portable boiling elements that can boil small quantities of water are useful when the right voltage of electricity is available.
